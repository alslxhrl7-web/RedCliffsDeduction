- Ground054: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ground054
- Wood049: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Wood049

Only the 1K-JPG Color and OpenGL normal maps are included. Roughness is deliberately
kept high via the Unity material smoothness setting; no displacement/tessellation is used.
Unity imports textures at a maximum of 1024 pixels, compressed, with mipmaps and
CPU read/write disabled. Download archives and unused maps stay outside Assets.

Tent folds, props and combined meshes are generated locally by CommandTentPolish.cs.
This folder does not contain a downloaded tent model or a Unity URP sample scene.
